How these prices are recorded

These are observed prices from a PC game session, saved to Supabase. They are not a global real-time market feed. Goods names use the English game localization. The timestamp shows when a price was received, not when it changed.

Each day's last observation counts once in the minimum, average and maximum. Days follow Europe/Berlin. Multiple logins on one day do not increase that day's weight. Unobserved days are not filled in, and the game's undated historical prices are not counted as dated observations.

A successful refresh can still show older observations if no new prices have been recorded. Refreshing this page never contacts the game server.
